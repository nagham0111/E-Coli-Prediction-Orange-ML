# Machine Learning-Based Prediction of E. coli Infection in Hospitalized Patients

This repository contains the data analysis workflow and implementation of a no-code machine learning framework developed to predict *Escherichia coli* infections in hospitalized patients using the **Orange Data Mining** platform.

 **Status:** Published in *Scientific Reports (Nature Portfolio)*  
 **Official Publication:** [View the full paper on Nature](https://nature.com)

---

##  Project Overview & Clinical Problem
Hospital-acquired infections (HAIs) are a critical global healthcare challenge, significantly increasing patient morbidity, mortality, and medical costs. Among nosocomial pathogens, ***Escherichia coli (E. coli)*** stands out as one of the most frequently isolated microorganisms, particularly driving urinary tract infections (UTIs), bloodstream infections, and surgical site infections.

Traditional microbiological culture methods require **24 to 72 hours** to finalize results. This delay frequently postpones targeted antibiotic therapy, leading to the overuse of empirical broad-spectrum antibiotics and accelerating antimicrobial resistance.

###  Objective
The primary goal of this project is to evaluate an interpretable, **no-code machine learning approach** to:
1. Predict *E. coli* infection early at the exact time of specimen collection.
2. Identify major clinical and demographic patient risk factors.
3. Support infection control teams and clinicians in rapid, data-driven decision-making before culture results are finalized.
